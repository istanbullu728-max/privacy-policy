1. Overview
This Privacy Policy and Terms of Service govern your use of the mobile application VocabStride ("the Application"), an English vocabulary learning platform. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when using our Service.

2. Third-Party Service Providers
VocabStride uses trusted third-party services to optimize learning analytics, handle user notifications, and process in-app subscriptions securely.

The third-party service providers integrated into the Application include:

Google Play Services: Essential for underlying operating system interactions and identity verification.

Google Analytics for Firebase: Used to monitor user engagement patterns, vocabulary learning progress analytics, and application performance metrics.

Firebase Cloud Messaging & Crashlytics: Used to deliver study reminders/notifications and identify stability issues or crashes.

RevenueCat: Used to securely process, validate, and manage in-app subscriptions and purchase receipts.

3. Log Data and Technical Information
When you use VocabStride, third-party SDKs may collect diagnostic log data in case of an error or crash. This data may include device IP addresses, device models, operating system versions, and usage timestamps. This data is strictly utilized for technical maintenance, analytics, and service enhancement.

4. Subscriptions and In-App Purchases (Terms of Use)
Auto-Renewable Subscriptions: VocabStride provides premium vocabulary learning features through auto-renewing subscriptions managed via Google Play In-App Billing and RevenueCat.

Billing: Payment will be charged to your Google Play Account at confirmation of purchase.

Renewal & Cancellation: Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renewal is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current billing cycle. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time via your Google Play Store Account Settings.
